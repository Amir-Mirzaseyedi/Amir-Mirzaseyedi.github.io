01About

I'm an Industrial Management graduate researcher at the University of Tehran, finishing an M.Sc. in Quality & Productivity Management (GPA 19.42/20). My work centers on how organizations evaluate and prioritize emerging technologies — especially AI — using structured multi-criteria decision-making methods (BWM, TOPSIS, WSM) and quality frameworks like TQM and EFQM. Most of my recent research applies these methods to banking service quality, technology investment prioritization, and digital transformation strategy, often paired with systematic reviews and expert-panel data collection.
